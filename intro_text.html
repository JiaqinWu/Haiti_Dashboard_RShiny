WELCOME TO Haiti HIV Dashboard

A dashboard for descriptive analysis of the condition of HIV patients in Haiti.

Dashboard enables intuitive, rapid and reproducible quality of description analysis of HIV patients without prior software experience. It facilitates understanding and communication of important trends, performances and patient outcome.

 WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH HAITI HIV DASHBOARD:

  • Explore the distribution of basic patient information in your selected options. 
  • Analyze the mean value of dispensation data for patients in your selected options. 
  • Examine the mean value of visit data for patients in your selected options. 
  • Investigate the distribution of patient diagnostic information in your selected options. 

If you need any help click the ? in the header. 

IMPORTANT: This dashboard uses HIV patient data in Haiti. This data was collected by CGHPI for internal usage.
